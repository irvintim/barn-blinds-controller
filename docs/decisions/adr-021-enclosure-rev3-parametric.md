# ADR-021: Enclosure Rev 3 is a generated, spreadsheet-driven model; two ADR-019 numbers corrected

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Accepted (corrects ADR-019's DIN hole spacing and Z-height requirement)

## Context

Rev 2 (`enclosure/rev2/project-case-rev2.FCStd`) has its dimensions typed
directly into 23 sketches, with no shared parameters. Fitting it to the Rev 1.3
board meant finding and changing dozens of hand-entered numbers, which is
the job we were facing. The Rev 1.3 board is the same 65 mm wide but 13.9 mm
longer (73.935 mm), has a 20-pin dual-row terminal block, and moves the
barrel jack, USB and blue LED.

Measuring the Rev 1.3 board for Rev 3 showed two things ADR-019 had wrong:

- **DIN clip holes.** The Phoenix USA 10 drawing dimensions **25 mm across the
  outer two of three holes**, a 12.5 mm pitch. ADR-019 and the old TODO said
  25 mm center to center (a 50 mm span).
- **Z height.** ADR-019 called for extra height for a "26.75 mm mated terminal
  block" (header 13.5 + plug 13.25 mm). J4 is right-angle, so the plug lies
  flat: header plus plug top out 13.65 mm above the board's underside, under
  Rev 2's ceiling of ~15.9 mm. The box does not need to grow taller.

## Decision

1. **Rev 3 is generated by `enclosure/rev3/build_case_rev3.py`** (FreeCAD 1.1,
   headless) into `project-case-rev3.FCStd`: two PartDesign bodies whose every
   dimension is an expression on one `Params` spreadsheet. After the first
   build, the spreadsheet in the FreeCAD GUI is where dimensions change.
2. **`check_case_rev3.py` is the fit gate.** It checks the model against the
   as-fabbed board STEP (`enclosure/reference/vsp-wsc4-rev1.3-board.step`) for
   interference and clearance, and re-solves with changed parameters to prove
   edits don't break it. It also exports STLs and preview renders.
3. Carry Rev 2's proven details forward with their dimensions: the
   split at the board top, the 0.55 mm tongue in a 0.8 mm groove, M3 screws
   from below into the lid posts, keyholes with an inside head channel, and the
   vent slots. Placements are re-derived from the Rev 1.3 board.
4. DIN clip holes use the drawing's 12.5 mm pitch. The rail runs along Y, so
   the terminal (+X) and USB (−X) walls face up and down on a rail.
5. Keyhole and DIN-boss positions are chosen to clear the board's underside
   parts (keyholes at x = ±13 mm; DIN bosses along y = −8 mm).

## Consequences

- A change of board or hardware is a spreadsheet edit plus a run of the checker.
- Corner-edge fillets reference edges by name, the one fragile spot in the
  tree. The checker's robustness pass covers it; if a GUI edit ever breaks a
  fillet, re-run the build script.
- Spreadsheet edits made in the GUI are lost if the build script is re-run.
  Either copy the edits into the script's `DEFAULTS` table or stop re-running it.
- ADR-019's mounting decision (wall keyholes + DIN clip from one box) stands;
  only the two numbers above are corrected.
